Directors’ pensions in 2026: what changed.

Three things changed in the last two years. Four questions show which apply to you.

Rules as at 24 September 2026. Budget 2027 is on 6 October 2026 and could change them.

Executive pensions

An executive pension is a company pension for one person. Those set up before 22 April 2021 had grace from stricter rules until 21 April 2026.

Now they must meet the full rules: two or more trustees (or a company trustee with two directors), written policies, and no new borrowing.

Most have moved to a master trust, a PRSA or a buy-out bond. A PRSA is not allowed after the scheme’s normal retirement age. The Pensions Authority counted 40,644 on 1 September 2026, down from 141,500 in January 2023.

Sources: the Pensions Authority’s notice of 23 March 2026 and its conference figures of 15 September 2026; Statutory Instrument (S.I.) No. 128 of 2021; Revenue Pensions Manual, chapter 13. A one-member scheme set up on or after 22 April 2021 has had to meet the full rules since 1 July 2022.

Company payments into a Personal Retirement Savings Account (PRSA)

Since 1 January 2025, your company can pay up to 100% of your pay from it into your PRSA each year, tax-free. Above that, you pay tax on the excess and the company cannot deduct it.

A master trust has no 100% test. Revenue limits it by salary, service and age.

Sources: Finance Act 2024, section 12; Revenue Pensions Manual, chapter 24.

The October window

What you pay in yourself, AVCs (additional voluntary contributions) included, can count against last year’s income. For 2025, pay by 31 October 2026, or 18 November 2026 if you pay and file on the Revenue Online Service (ROS).

Company payments cannot be backdated. The company’s year end is the deadline.

Sources: Revenue Pensions Manual, appendix III and chapters 4 and 24; Revenue eBrief 034/26.

The Standard Fund Threshold

The cap on pension savings with full tax relief is €2.2 million in 2026, rising €200,000 a year to €2.8 million in 2029, then with earnings. The €500,000 limit on lump sums taxed at 20% or less stays.

Small self-administered schemes

These still need a Revenue-approved pensioneer trustee. They cannot lend to members or the company, or buy property from the company or let property to it.

Sources: Revenue Pensions Manual, chapter 19; Pensions Act 1990, section 61B.

Four questions

Do you have a company pension set up for you alone before 22 April 2021?
Would you like your company to pay in more than your pay from it in a year?
Is your scheme’s normal retirement age close, or already past?
Could all your pensions together come near €2 million?
