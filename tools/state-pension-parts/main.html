How it is worked out

  • A full record, 2,080, gets the maximum personal rate: €299.30 a week from January 2026. From 520 to 2,080 it is pro rata.
  • To qualify you need 520 paid contributions. Credited contributions (counted up to 520) and HomeCaring Periods do not count toward it. The tool does not check your mix. The means-tested State Pension (Non-Contributory) is not calculated.
  • Until 2034 the Department of Social Protection pays the higher of two methods. This tool uses one, the Total Contributions Approach, so below the maximum the figure is a floor: you may get more. The State Pension entitlement check does both.
  • The personal rate at 66, without increases for a qualified adult or child, or at 80. A year is 52 weekly payments.
  • Living standards are for a single person, in 2024 prices.
