How we worked this out
  • Not included: your old pensions, your tax position, your employer’s scheme.
  • A full record of 2,080 gets the maximum personal rate, €299.30 a week from January 2026, and €309.30 from January 2027 (Budget 2027). Between 520 and 2,080 the rate is pro rata.
  • Credited contributions count up to 520. This tool does not check how your total is made up.
  • The qualifying minimum is 520 paid contributions, ten years. Credited contributions and HomeCaring Periods do not count toward it. The means-tested State Pension (Non-Contributory) may apply instead. This tool does not calculate it.
  • Until 2034 the Department of Social Protection pays the higher of two calculations. Method 1 is the Total Contributions Approach alone. Method 2 combines it with the Yearly Average method. This tool shows Method 1 only. The State Pension entitlement check does both.
  • So a figure below the maximum is a floor. You may be paid more, and the gap may be smaller. At 2,080 the figure is exact.
  • The rate shown is the personal rate at 66. It excludes increases for a qualified adult or child, and the higher rate from 80. Rates change, usually at each Budget. A year is 52 weekly payments.
  • The living standards are for a single person. They are annual spending in 2024 prices, not adjusted for inflation since. Couple figures are not shown. Both sides move over time, and not together.
