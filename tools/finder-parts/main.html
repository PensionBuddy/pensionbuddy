    What this is, and what it is not

    • Free to start, with no obligation. Finding a pension is not the same as moving it.
    • We ask, the providers answer. We cannot promise every pension will be found: schemes close, merge and change hands, and old records are not always complete.
    • Nothing is moved or changed. The letter allows questions, not transactions.
    • Your details are used for the search. Nothing else, unless you ask for more.

    Good to know

    • Moving a pension is a separate decision, for later and with advice. A transfer can mean giving up terms you cannot get back, such as the pension a defined benefit scheme promised, which is swapped for a transfer value. So it is never assumed.
    • This finder is for pensions from work and personal pensions. Your State Pension record is on MyWelfare.ie, and the State Pension entitlement check works through it.
    • Worked in the UK? The UK government’s Pension Tracing Service finds contact details for UK schemes, free.
    • You can withdraw the letter at any time, in writing, and we stop asking.
    • Prefer to start on paper? The old pension hunt checklist sets out the steps, and prints.
    • Once a pension is found, its charges are worth knowing. The pension charges calculator shows what they can take out of a pot by retirement.

    This is a search, not advice. We gather what your old providers hold and tell you what it is. Any advice about what to do with a pension is given in a personal consultation with Damian, if you want one.
