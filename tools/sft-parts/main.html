The rules

  • Finance Act 2024, section 13, sets the threshold. It was €2 million from 2014 to 2025. From 2030 it tracks Central Statistics Office average weekly earnings and cannot fall.
  • Each benefit is tested against all taken since 7 December 2005. Here, all are taken in one year.
  • Chargeable excess tax: any excess is taxed at 40% when taken, with no reliefs (Revenue). Up to €60,000 of 20% lump sum tax can be set against it.
  • Figures are illustrations, not a guarantee.
