How we worked this out
  • Each month the pot grows at 5% a year, before charges: risk level 4 of 7, about half shares and half bonds, a mix we chose for illustration. Then a twelfth of the yearly charge comes out. Then the payment goes in, less the charge on payments. Growth is steady and the same for both plans; real returns differ between funds and can be negative.
  • Your plan starts at 1% a year and 5% of each payment, the most a Standard Personal Retirement Savings Account (PRSA) may charge under the Pensions Act. Other plans can charge more, or in other ways.
  • Not included: policy fees, set-up charges, bid/offer spreads on older contracts, exit or switching charges, the terms an older plan may carry, your tax relief. A plan with them costs more. The Competition and Consumer Protection Commission (CCPC) explains each in its guide to pension fees and charges.
  • Payments stay level until you retire. The main figures ignore inflation; the line under each gives today’s money, with prices rising 2% a year (the European Central Bank’s target).
