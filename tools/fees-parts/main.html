How we worked this out
  • Not included: policy, set-up and exit charges, the terms an older plan may carry, your tax relief.
  • Each month the pot grows at your yearly rate, before charges. Then a twelfth of the annual management charge comes out. Then the payment goes in, less the charge on payments.
  • Your plan’s defaults, 1% a year and 5% of each payment, are the most a Standard Personal Retirement Savings Account (PRSA) may charge under the Pensions Act. Other plans can charge more, or in other ways.
  • Not included: policy fees, set-up charges, bid/offer spreads on older contracts, exit or switching charges. A plan with them costs more. The Competition and Consumer Protection Commission (CCPC) explains each in its guide to pension fees and charges.
  • Payments stay level until you retire. The main figures ignore inflation; the line under each gives today’s money, with prices rising 2% a year (the European Central Bank’s target). Figures ignore tax relief. Relief changes what a payment costs you, not what the charges take.
  • Growth is steady and the same for both plans. Real returns also differ between funds.
