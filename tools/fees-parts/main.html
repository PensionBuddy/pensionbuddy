How we worked this out
  • Each month the pot grows at 6% a year, before charges: the most a pension projection may assume. Then a twelfth of the yearly charge comes out. Then the payment goes in, less the charge on payments. Growth is steady and the same for both plans; real returns differ between funds and can be negative.
  • Your plan starts at 0.75% a year and nothing on payments, the plan you compare it with at 0.4% a year: examples, not any provider’s charges. The most a Standard Personal Retirement Savings Account (PRSA) may charge under the Pensions Act is 1% a year and 5% of each payment. Some older plans charge more, or in other ways.
  • Not included: policy fees, set-up charges, bid/offer spreads on older contracts, exit or switching charges, the terms an older plan may carry, your tax relief. A plan with them costs more. The Competition and Consumer Protection Commission (CCPC) explains each in its guide to pension fees and charges.
  • Payments stay level until you retire.
