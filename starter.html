    Your rate of income tax

    €100 a month into your pension, at the 20% rate of income tax:

    It costs you€80

    Revenue adds€20

    Relief is at your rate of income tax, within Revenue’s limit: 15% of earnings under 30, rising with age to 40% from 60, on earnings up to €115,000.

    Auto-enrolment. Three pay in.

    Most €100 €50

    If you put in €50 a month:

    You put in €50
    Your employer adds €50
    The State adds €17
    In all, a month €117

    Auto-enrolment takes up to €100 a month from you.

    That’s the most auto-enrolment takes. A personal pension takes more, with tax relief. Compare the two
