€1,000 of profit taken as salary is about €477 in your pocket, after 40% income tax, 8% USC and 4.35% PRSI (the rate from 1 October 2026). Left as profit, 12.5% corporation tax takes €125. Into your pension, all €1,000 goes in, within Revenue’s limits; it is taxed when you take it, usually from 60, apart from a lump sum that is tax-free within limits.     How we worked this out
    • The company pays the same amount every year, monthly, from now to 66. What you have in pensions now grows at the same rate.
    • Growth: 6% a year before charges, the most the Pensions Authority’s guidance lets a projection assume. The figures are in euro at the time, before inflation.
    • A year later: the same yearly payment, starting a year late and still stopping at 66: one year’s payments fewer, and their growth.
    • As salary: 40% income tax, 8% Universal Social Charge (USC) and 4.35% Pay-Related Social Insurance (PRSI), as for someone already paying the top rates. Employer PRSI left out.
    • Left as profit: corporation tax at 12.5%, the trading rate.
    • Tax relief on your own payments: 15% of earnings under 30, up to 40% at 60 and over, on earnings up to €115,000. The company’s payments are not capped by these percentages.
    • Personal Retirement Savings Account (PRSA): the company’s payments are tax-free for you up to 100% of your pay from the company that year (since 1 January 2025). Above that, the excess is a taxable benefit-in-kind and the company can’t deduct it.
    • A company scheme such as a master trust: not held to the 100% test. Revenue’s maximum funding rules limit it, by salary, service and age; an actuary works it out.
    • Left out: charges, and the tax when you take the pension.
    • Standard Fund Threshold: €2.2 million in 2026, rising €200,000 a year to €2.8 million in 2029, then with earnings; any excess is taxed at 40% when taken.
    • Sources: Revenue, tax rates and bands, USC and Corporation Tax rates (Taxes Consolidation Act 1997, section 21A); Revenue Pensions Manual, chapters 4 and 24; Finance Act 2024, sections 12 and 13; the Pensions Authority; the Department of Social Protection, SW14.

    Executive pension set up before 22 April 2021? Since 22 April 2026 it must meet the full rules for company schemes.
