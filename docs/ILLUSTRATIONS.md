# Where painted illustrations would replace icons

Run 39, for Damian: the places on the site where a small painted
illustration (hand-made, in the house colours, Buddy where he fits) would
replace a line icon or a plain number, to make the site feel made by
people. A list to brief an illustrator from; nothing on the site has
changed. Sizes are as the icons are drawn today, at 1440 and at 375
(measured on 1 October 2026); paint at least twice that, for sharp
screens.

## Leave as they are

- **The "information, not advice" box** on every calculator and guide (its
  "i" icon), and anything else in a warning, the regulator line or the
  review line: these are read for what they say, and a painting would
  decorate a warning.
- **The paw mark** (the logo, and the paw behind the final band): it is
  the brand, and Buddy's own.
- **Working icons:** search, the menu, close, the arrows on buttons and
  links, play and pause on the game videos, the jargon chips. They have to
  be read at a glance, the same everywhere.
- **The 404's Buddy, the founders' photographs, the game pictures:** they
  are photographs and the games' own art already.
- **The directors page's eight cards** had icons, hidden in an earlier run;
  they read well without. Paintings could go there later, but they would be
  new pictures, not replacements.

## For the illustrator

- Paint on a transparent ground; the site sits them on white and on its
  palest teal. The house teals are #0C8175, #08655A and #0A332E; the ink
  is #0B1F1C.
- No words or figures inside a painting: the words beside it say it, and
  words in a picture cannot be read aloud or translated.
- Each one is decorative (the text beside it carries the meaning), so it
  needs no description for a screen reader.
- Delivered as PNG with transparency, at least twice the size above; the
  site makes its own WebP copies and keeps each one's box before it loads.
