Good to know

Questions, answered.

I don't have any of the paperwork, is that a problem?+

Not at all. Roughly when you worked somewhere, and who for, is usually enough to start tracing a pension.

Can I combine my old pensions into one?+

Often, yes, and it makes them far easier to keep track of. But it isn't always right. Some older pensions carry valuable guarantees worth keeping, so we'll look at each one and tell you honestly.

Is it really worth the hassle?+

There usually is money there. Knowing what you've got, and making sure it's invested rather than sitting forgotten, can make a real difference.
