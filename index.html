Revenue deadline

Contributions for the 2025 tax year close on 31 October.

Pay into a pension before the deadline and you can set it against last year’s tax bill. Miss it and that year is gone for good. If you file your return online, you usually have until mid‑November.

Applies to anyone claiming relief for the 2025 tax year, including AVCs, self-assessed income and company directors.

Our story

Father, son, dog.

One idea: a pension should be something you understand, not something you avoid.

Pensionbuddy started as a father-son conversation. Damian has spent 30 years advising people on pensions, and kept seeing the same thing: people either ignored their pension completely, or only thought about it the day auto-enrolment came into effect.

Adam, freshly out of Trinity, saw the same gap from the other side. Nobody was explaining any of it in a fun and digestible way that actually made sense. So they built Pensionbuddy together.

The goal is simple: get you from “I’ve no idea what’s happening with my pension” to an actual conversation with someone qualified to help.

B.A., Q.F.A.
Founder and CEO, QFA

Damian Condon

I’ve spent thirty years explaining how money can best serve real people. Clarity is the whole point.

After graduating from Maynooth, I moved from Bank of Ireland to Ulster Bank, then to St. George Private Bank in Sydney. I founded Gresham Wealth Management in 2012 and have led it ever since, and I am the Qualified Financial Adviser behind Pensionbuddy.

Thirty years of that work leaves you with one firm opinion: hardly anyone is bad with money. They have just never had it explained to them in words they felt allowed to ask questions about.
