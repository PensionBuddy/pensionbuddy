    Revenue’s deadline

    Revenue’s deadline is 18 November if you pay and file online.

    If you do not pay and file online through the Revenue Online Service, it is 31 October. Pay in before then to claim against last year’s tax.

    Your pension through life

    Key dates for your pension.

    Under 30 15% Tax relief on up to 15% of your pay. 1875
    40

    From 40: Tax relief on up to 25% of your pay.

    1. Under 30

      Tax relief on up to 15% of your pay. Pay over €115,000 a year doesn’t count, at any age.

    2. From 30

      Tax relief on up to 20% of your pay.

    3. From 40

      Tax relief on up to 25% of your pay.

    4. From 50

      Tax relief on up to 30% of your pay. A pension from a past job can be taken now, if its rules allow.

    5. From 55

      Tax relief on up to 35% of your pay.

    6. From 60

      Tax relief on up to 40% of your pay, the most there is. A Personal Retirement Savings Account (PRSA) or a personal pension can normally be taken now.

    7. From 61

      If your pension is in an Approved Retirement Fund (ARF), you’re taxed as if you took out 4% a year, even if you take nothing.

    8. At 66

      The State Pension starts: up to €15,564 a year.

    9. From 71

      In an ARF, you’re taxed as if you took out 5% a year, up from 4%.

    Rules as at 8 October 2026.
