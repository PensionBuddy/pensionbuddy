    Your pension through life

    What changes, and when.

    15% Under 30 1875
    40

    25% From 40

    1. Under 30

      Tax relief on up to 15% of earnings, capped at €115,000.

    2. From 30

      20% from 30 to 39.

    3. From 40

      25% from 40 to 49.

    4. From 50

      30% from 50 to 54. A pension from a past job can be taken from 50, if its rules allow.

    5. From 55

      35% from 55 to 59.

    6. From 60

      40% from 60 on. A Personal Retirement Savings Account (PRSA) and a personal pension are normally taken from 60.

    7. From 61

      An Approved Retirement Fund (ARF) is taxed on at least 4% a year, taken or not.

    8. At 66

      Pension age. The State Pension pays €15,564 a year at the maximum, €16,084 from January 2027.

    9. From 71

      The ARF is taxed on at least 5% a year.

    Rules as at 8 October 2026.

    Revenue’s deadline

    Revenue’s deadline is 18 November if you pay and file online.

    If you do not pay and file online through the Revenue Online Service, it is 31 October. Pay in before then to claim against last year’s tax.

    For relief on the 2025 tax year, additional voluntary contributions (AVCs) and company directors included.

    €100 a month

    It could cost you about €60 at the higher rate of tax, or €80 at the standard rate, within Revenue’s age-related limits.
