What usually happens

Nobody decides to ignore their pension.
It just gets away from them.

If one of these lands, you are in very ordinary company. It is also fixable.

Scattered

You’ve had four jobs. How many pensions?

Most people cannot answer that. The money is still yours, but nobody is minding it, and nobody is going to ring you about it.

Not started

Waiting is the most expensive thing you can do

A year of delay is not just a year lost. It is the year that had the longest left to grow, and it does not come back.

Left on the table

Your company may be paying tax it doesn’t need to

Company contributions can go well beyond personal limits and reduce corporation tax. Most directors work this out years later than they’d like.

Revenue deadline

Contributions for the 2025 tax year close on 31 October.

Pay into a pension before the deadline and you can set it against last year’s tax bill. Miss it and that year is gone for good. You cannot go back and use it later. If you file your return online, you usually have until mid‑November.

Applies to anyone claiming relief for the 2025 tax year, including AVCs, self-assessed income and company directors.
