    This check is information only. It is not a suitability assessment and not advice: it adds up your answers and does not look at your finances. Nothing you answer is sent anywhere or stored. If you book a call, the booking page only knows which of the three situations you picked.

    How the score works

    • Each of the five questions after the first is worth up to 20 points: 20 for the best answer, 10 for a partial one, and 0 otherwise. The first question only chooses which five you are asked.
    • Only things you can find out or do are scored. Paying in on your own scores the same as paying in with an employer, and being self-employed scores the same as knowing what an employer would add.
    • 0 to 39 is early days, 40 to 74 is on the way, and 75 to 100 is in good shape. The score is a checklist added up, not a measure of how much you have saved.
